## RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES

### Risk 1: Employee Resistance to Communal Living

**Mitigation:**
- Phase-in approach: voluntary participation Year 1 (offer premium to testers)
- Private family units (not dormitory style)
- Democratic governance of residential rules
- Market-rate salary option for those preferring external living
- Regular satisfaction surveys + rapid iteration

### Risk 2: Agricultural Production Variability (Drought, Pests)

**Mitigation:**
- Diversified crop portfolio
- Drought-resistant varieties (South African indigenous crops)
- Insurance coverage for catastrophic loss
- 6-month food reserve buffer
- Greenhouses for controlled environment (20% of production)

### Risk 3: Component Supply Chain Disruption

**Mitigation:**
- 3-month inventory buffer for critical components
- Dual-sourcing for all externally-sourced parts
- Rapid prototyping capability (design around available components)
- Strategic partnerships with SADC manufacturers
- Government advocacy for regional electronics manufacturing incentives

### Risk 4: Regulatory/Tax Complications of EFE Model

**Mitigation:**
- Legal consultation from Day 1 (tax specialists, labor law experts)
- SARS engagement: classify housing/food as "benefits in kind" (taxed appropriately)
- Full transparency: all transactions documented
- Compliance buffer: 5% of profit allocated to regulatory/legal costs
- Political advocacy: demonstrate model to government as poverty reduction tool

### Risk 5: Market Competition from Larger Players (Samsung, Xiaomi)

**Mitigation:**
- Differentiation on ethics/sustainability (not just price)
- Premium positioning for export markets
- Government procurement focus (local content requirements)
- Network effects: employees as brand ambassadors
- Continuous internalization → perpetual cost advantage

### Risk 6: Technology Obsolescence (5G, 6G transitions)

**Mitigation:**
- R&D allocation: 3-5% of profit to innovation
- University partnerships (UCT, Wits, Stellenbosch)
- Open-source contriions (RISC-V, Linux mobile)
- Modular design philosophy (easier upgrades)
- Licensing agreements with tech leaders (short-term)
