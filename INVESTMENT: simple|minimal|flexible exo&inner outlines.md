**Intul's Investment Structure:**

1. **Factories produce for TOTAL supply** (local + export)
2. **Local portion = free to EquiNation members**
3. **Export portion = Intul's international sales**

**How it works:**
- Intul builds capacity to meet local demand PLUS export targets
- [note: not sure this point is neccessary though...] Local allocation comes "first" (covered by EC/pledges for operational costs)
- Everything beyond local needs = Intul's export profit
- Intul gets: no salary costs, motivated workforce, operational funding via EC
- EquiNation gets: free processors locally, jobs, technology transfer

**AVOID "buy back" where possible, preference supply** - that creates unnecessary complexity

**Instead:**
- Factory outputs X processors/month
- Y go to local market (free)
- (X - Y) go to international market (Intul's profit)
- Simple, clean split

**Scale the factory to:**
1. Cover 100% local demand
2. PLUS whatever export volume Intul wants

**Benefits to Intul:**
- Zero salary overhead
- Subsidized operations (EC covers local costs)
- Price flexibility on exports (near-zero production cost)
- Can dominate markets or maximize profits

**Key principle:** 
International investment works BEST when factories are scaled to serve both local (free) and export (profit) simultaneously, not as separate operations.

**FRAMEWORK: Three Investment Models**

**1. INTERNATIONAL CORP INVESTMENT (Intul Model)**
- They build infrastructure at scale (local + export capacity)
- Benefits: Zero salaries, EC-funded operations, motivated workforce
- Local output: FREE to members
- Export output: 100% profit to investor
- *Best for: Tech, manufacturing, large-scale production*

**2. SIBLING COMPANY MODEL**
- International company creates local subsidiary
- Fully integrates into EFE economy
- Operates like any local business (EC, pledges, free local provision)
- Parent company benefits from export profits funneled up
- *Best for: Services, retail, localized production*

**3. LOCAL COMPANY + INTERNATIONAL FUNDING**
- Local entrepreneur starts company
- Seeks international investment (capital injection)
- Investor gets ROI from export profits
- Company operates in EFE framework (free local, profit export)
- *Best for: Startups, innovation, specialized production*

---

**UNIVERSAL PRINCIPLES (apply to all 3):**

✓ Local provision = FREE (or import-cost only)
✓ Export sales = PROFIT (to investor)
✓ No salary burden
✓ EC/pledges cover operational costs
✓ Investor ROI comes from EXPORTS, not local sales

---

**FLEXIBILITY SCALE:**
- 10% investment → 10% of export profits
- 100% investment → 100% of export profits
- Partial investment → Proportional export profit share

**ECONOMY PROTECTION:**
- Local access remains free regardless of investment level
- Investor profit comes from EXTERNAL markets only
- This ensures: Investment ALWAYS benefits economy (jobs, products, internalization) while NEVER harming local free access

**The Golden Rule:**
*Investors profit from EXPORTS. Locals benefit from FREE ACCESS. These never conflict.*

LOCAL BUSINESS BENEFITS (Operating in EFE)
Core Advantages:

✓ Zero salary costs (all staff receive EDS)
✓ Free/low-cost local inputs (internalized supply chain)
✓ Operational funding (EC from employees or pledges)
✓ No local pricing pressure (provision is free anyway)
✓ Export = 100% profit (near-zero production costs)

What They Get:

Motivated workforce (working by choice, not necessity)
Community support (pledges for expansion)
Price flexibility on exports (dominate markets OR maximize profit)
Innovation freedom (failure doesn't = bankruptcy)
Quality focus (not cost-cutting pressure)

Best For: Any business - from solo artisan to major manufacturer

LOCAL BUSINESS INVESTING EXTERNALLY
Two Models:
1. DIRECT EXTERNAL EXPANSION

Use accumulated export profits to invest abroad
Establish operations in traditional economies
Advantage: lower costs enable aggressive pricing
Profit returns to EFE treasury → increases all member values

2. PARTNERSHIP/JOINT VENTURE

Partner with international businesses
Provide products/components at competitive prices
Export revenue strengthens home economy
Can gradually introduce EFE concepts to partners

Key Benefits:

Capital from export profits (not loans)
Cost advantage (no salary burden at home)
Quality products (motivated workforce)
Good low market entry creating sizable market OR price at market for maximum profit
Strengthens national economy through returning profits

Strategic Approach:

Internalize locally first (free access at home)
Build export strength (accumulate capital)
Use profits to expand internationally
Prosper in external markets too
Profits flow back, benefiting all members

Golden Rule: External investment ALWAYS returns profit to home treasury, benefiting entire EFE economy.
